# Learning Model

Learners progress through repeated cycles: model a goal, practise in conversation, reflect on one or two priorities, retry, and revisit through spaced scenarios. Level placement is learner-selected in the MVP and can later be assisted by an assessment.

| Level | Objectives | Vocabulary range | Grammar coverage | Conversation complexity | Progress criteria |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Starter | Give personal details and basic needs | 300–500 words | `be`, pronouns, articles, simple present, basic questions | 1–2 sentence guided turns | Completes familiar exchanges with prompts and mostly understandable meaning |
| Beginner | Handle routine daily exchanges | 600–1,000 | present/past basics, `can`, imperatives, common prepositions | Short guided dialogues | Sustains 4–6 turns and uses core patterns consistently |
| Elementary | Describe experiences and plans | 1,200–1,800 | past forms, future forms, comparatives, countability, conjunctions | Familiar scenarios with follow-ups | Sustains 2–3 minutes with recoverable errors and less prompting |
| Pre-Intermediate | Explain opinions and solve common problems | 2,000–3,000 | present perfect, modals, conditionals basics, relative clauses | Multi-step social/workplace tasks | Gives connected answers, self-corrects, and transfers feedback across scenarios |
| Intermediate | Participate confidently in everyday and professional discussion | 3,500–5,000 | tense control, passive, reported speech, broader conditionals, discourse markers | Less predictable discussions and negotiation | Sustains 5–8 minutes with clear organization and few meaning-blocking errors |

Vocabulary ranges are directional receptive/working targets, not hard gates. Advancement uses a rolling portfolio: scenario completion, comprehensibility, independence, recurring-error improvement, vocabulary use, and fluency. Accent conformity is never a criterion.

## Daily selection and evaluation

The catalogue contains two initial lessons per level. Selection matches level, then prefers goal-aligned scenarios, incomplete lessons, and lessons outside the three most recent sessions. Ties are sorted by ID and indexed by UTC date. Text evaluation transparently scores grammar, vocabulary variety, response length, completed turns, and participation; it is a coaching proxy and never a pronunciation assessment.
