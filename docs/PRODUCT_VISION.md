# Product Vision

## Vision

Give Indian learners a private, always-available place to become confident English speakers through realistic voice conversations. The first experience is designed for Telugu-speaking learners, while the underlying learning model remains language-independent.

## Audience

Primary users are Telugu-speaking adults, college students, job seekers, and working professionals with basic English knowledge but low speaking confidence. Secondary users are students aged 12+, homemakers, and interview candidates.

## Value proposition

Learners practise without a teacher, receive corrections at natural pauses rather than after every sentence, and advance through structured levels. The tutor develops grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, fluency, and confidence, offering concise Telugu explanations only when requested or confusion is detected.

## Principles and outcomes

- Voice first, with text as an accessibility and reliability fallback.
- Psychological safety before technical perfection.
- Useful communication before accent imitation.
- A small number of actionable corrections beats exhaustive criticism.
- Progress is demonstrated through increasingly independent conversation.

Success means learners speak more often and for longer, need fewer prompts, repeat priority errors less, and confidently handle everyday and professional scenarios.
