# MVP Scope

## Included

- Simple learner identity foundation and future email/password boundary; authentication itself is deferred.
- Proficiency selection across five levels.
- One daily speaking lesson and scenario-based conversations.
- Daily Conversation, Workplace English, Job Interview, Travel, Shopping, Doctor Visit, Telephone Conversation, and Free Talk modes.
- Text fallback and provider-neutral voice input architecture.
- Tutor replies, transcript display, and delayed correction summaries.
- Grammar corrections, vocabulary suggestions, fluency feedback, and confidence-supporting praise.
- Progress history and a daily streak.
- A limited free plan with subscription-ready entitlements.

## Explicitly excluded

Live human tutors, social networking, video lessons, multiplayer practice, complex gamification, school/enterprise dashboards, languages beyond Telugu support, production payments, production speech/LLM providers, native web/mobile frontends, and production operations.

## MVP completion signal

A learner can select a level, complete a text-or-voice-shaped conversation lesson, see a transcript and useful delayed feedback, and later review progress. Provider boundaries may initially use deterministic local implementations; no paid service is required to develop or test the system.
